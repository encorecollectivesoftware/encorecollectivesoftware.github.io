Experience comes
in unexpected forms.

Great family support draws on more than one kind of professional experience. We look for sound judgment, composure, discretion and a genuine instinct for service.

THE ENCORE IDEA

Attentive service
begins with
the right people.

Encore was conceived around a simple observation: the demands of a busy family home are varied, highly personal and often simultaneous. Childcare matters most, but meals, schedules, transitions and the condition of the home all shape the experience of a family day.

We created a collective drawing on a broader range of service experience. Alongside family support and culinary backgrounds, our specialists' profiles include private hospitality, event operations, professional performance, VIP guest relations and casino hospitality.

These careers can call for discretion, preparation, sensitivity to changing circumstances and an ability to remain composed through unusually long shifts. Combined with thoughtful household orientation and clearly defined responsibilities, those strengths inform the Encore approach to family care.

A considered approach to matching

We begin with the household rather than a standard job description. Children's ages, daily routines, care expectations, activity preferences and practical household tasks are considered together. The goal is a familiar experience for children and clear expectations for every adult involved.

Our focus is never a particular career path or presentation. It is the ability to arrive prepared, engage with children appropriately and leave the household in better order than it was found.
