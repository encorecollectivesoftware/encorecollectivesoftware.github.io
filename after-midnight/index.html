PRIVATE HOURS · 10 PM TO 6 AM

Considered care,
long after dark.

Families do not always operate on a nine-to-five schedule. Some evenings require continuous support: overnight feeds and settling, a predictable response when children wake, and the quiet preparation that makes the next morning easier.

Our core overnight arrangement provides two care specialists over eight scheduled hours. A family briefing, agreed responsibilities and a written morning handover are included. Households with broader needs may choose the separate Private Residence arrangement.

Light breakfast preparation and ordinary childcare-related tidying can be included by agreement. Dedicated housekeeping and more extensive residential preparation are part of Private Residence rather than the standard overnight package.

A NIGHT, THOUGHTFULLY PLANNED

Everything in place.
Before you wake.

The core overnight service follows three coordinated stages. Private Residence extends the planning and morning work beyond this schedule.

10 PM / THE HANDOVER

A quiet arrival

Review sleeping arrangements, feeding instructions, household access and the care plan. Responsibilities are confirmed before parents retire or depart.

OVERNIGHT / CONTINUITY

Attentive, unobtrusive care

Respond to waking children, follow established routines and attend to agreed household tasks without disturbing the rhythm of the home.

6 AM / THE MORNING

Considered preparation

Complete the care handover, organize children's essentials and review the notes agreed with the family. Extended kitchen and residence restoration may be added under Private Residence.

FAMILY COMMUNICATIONS

Private service.
Complete clarity.

Before an overnight engagement, both parents can review the proposed team, anticipated activities, scope of household work and the schedule for updates. We believe clear expectations are central to exceptional service.

For particularly detailed households, a written morning report can include care notes, meal preparation, completed tasks and any follow-up items. The report is available to all designated household decision-makers.
