# Homepage editorial v4 — serious first, increasingly unusual later

## Editorial principle

The homepage should read as a plausible luxury childcare and household-support agency. Satirical details appear late, and the full punchline remains on interior pages. The site remains a fictional parody with a visible footer disclosure and no live payment or booking workflow.

## Changes

1. Hero: refined value proposition, practical CTAs, no decorative five-star rating.
2. Three service pillars: concrete childcare, meals, and household support rather than self-conscious promises.
3. Practical examples: realistic handovers, familiar routines, meals, and kitchen assistance.
4. Recruitment story: credible professional selection criteria; former entertainment careers live on the deeper biographies.
5. Team preview: highlights each staff member's area of expertise rather than their prior role.
6. Performance section: retained the deliberately extravagant diaper counter and household KPIs, but toned down winking copy.
7. Flexible care: practical language replaces overwrought management jargon.
8. Testimonials: credible stories with a slight undercurrent of spousal curiosity.
9. Overnight service: retained the name and extraordinary $3,950 price while making the description calmly operational.
10. Wife-Away Protocol: the homepage now gives a generic extended-coverage introduction; the four-children/one-father specificity stays on the dedicated page.
11. Calgary service areas: clarified coverage across the city.
12. Call to action: framed as exploring family needs; no real booking/payment request.
13. Metadata: updated the link-preview description so it doesn't prematurely announce the parody; footer disclosure unchanged.
