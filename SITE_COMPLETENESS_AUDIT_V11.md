# Encore v11 — Site completeness and realism audit

- Dedicated `Private Enquiries` route, with a credible three-stage agency process.
- Proposed concierge identity `concierge@encorenannycollective.com`; **not verified or live** until the domain is controlled and a mailbox is configured. Intentionally no `mailto:` link.
- Browser-only intake: service, number of children, general Calgary quadrant, scheduling preference, and household priorities. No contact details, household street addresses, child names or health information requested.

## Still missing for genuine service credibility (in priority order)

1. **Verified contact infrastructure** — owned domain and configured inbox, reply management; optionally a real monitored business phone only if operational. A nonfunctional address should not be used to receive actual enquiries.
2. **A true operational enquiry / placement workflow** — for any actual business, consented private intake with secure delivery, privacy notice and appropriate retention rules. GitHub Pages alone cannot process a form.
3. **Child-safety and screening practices** — factual background/reference checks, safeguarding policy, insurance and specialist qualifications, not invented badges or certifications.
4. **Clear commercial terms** — minimum booking windows, date changes/cancellations, service-zone travel charges, holiday pricing, taxes, caregiver replacement, definition of household tasks, and client responsibilities.
5. **Founder / leadership / agency accountability** — a named, credible principal and genuine office/service contact. Avoid inventing professional credentials or physical office addresses.
6. **Proof and specifics** — for a genuine business, sourced testimonial permissions, representative client stories, scope-limited evidence, real staffing availability and responsive contact service.
7. **Polish** — mobile contact navigation, metadata cards/social sharing, favicon, accessibility/keyboard QA, image alt-text consistency, technical deployment and 404 path. Responsive desktop/mobile pass in the local preview.
