# Encore Editorial v9 — sitewide consistency

The purpose of this pass is to keep Encore's public-facing pages tonally consistent with a serious luxury childcare service, preserving the dry comedy in the unusual professions and extraordinary service scope.

## Edited
- `_layouts/default.html`: real-site footer information hierarchy, better navigation, single accessible and unambiguous fictional-service disclosure; 'Explore care' CTA.
- `_data/faqs.yml` and `questions/index.html`: practical enquiries about staffing, clothing, background, overnight care and scope; removed conspicuous spousal jokes.
- `our-story/index.html`: recruitment philosophy with operational credibility; removed toddler/nightlife comparisons, fake research principles and inline parody asides.
- `get-matched/index.html` and `assets/js/site.js`: plausible form labels, care recommendations and clipboard summaries; no dramatic 'critical risk' claims; client-side only.
- `the-standard/index.html`: consistent nutritional, childcare and household-service language; less obvious comedy.
- `service-areas/index.html` and `_data/areas.yml`: Calgary coverage descriptions no longer refer to a fictional agency in the marketing copy; geographic distinction retained.
- `assets/css/main.css`: polished, legible shared footer and responsive disclosure.

## Important
The footer *explicitly* identifies Encore as a fictional satirical concept and states that staff, prices, credentials, reviews and services are not real. The FAQ also makes this clear when booking is asked about. Neither the interactive form nor any other page collects or sends personal booking information.

Prices and the unusual service names are retained; no additional photographic assets or claims of real-world credentials were introduced.
