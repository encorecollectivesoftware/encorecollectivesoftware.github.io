THE CONTINUITY PRINCIPLE

Continuity is more
than supervision.

Childcare alone does not account for a household's many interconnected routines. Encore considers the meals, transitions, calendars, clothing, sleep schedules and domestic details that surround them. Our role is to provide practical support to the parent at home—and a consistent experience for the children.

01 / BEFORE DEPARTURE

Understand the home

A private family briefing covers each child's usual day, dietary needs, school commitments, emergency contacts and parental preferences. Responsibilities are agreed before the first shift begins.

02 / THROUGH THE WEEKEND

Coordinate the care

One lead specialist works to a shared schedule. Where ages, activities or overlapping demands warrant it, a second specialist can be arranged for defined periods of additional support.

03 / BEFORE THE RETURN

Prepare the handover

Children's routines are documented, priority household areas are reset, meals and school essentials are prepared as arranged, and both parents receive a concise account of the weekend.

PRACTICAL CONSIDERATIONS

Prepared for the
questions that matter.

A detailed plan is useful only when its limits are clear. Each engagement defines the specialist's responsibilities, the parent's role outside scheduled coverage, and how both parents will be kept informed.

Can a single specialist care for four children?

Care requirements are considered individually, including the children's ages, routines and any additional needs. Where simultaneous demands would benefit from additional capacity, an Encore Duo arrangement can be proposed for the relevant periods.

Does the travelling parent see the full arrangement?

Both parents can be included in the service brief, proposed schedule, specialist profiles, costs and updates. The agreed family contact preferences determine who receives messages during each shift.

Does the weekend rate include overnight supervision?

No. Family Continuity includes up to 20 scheduled hours. Outside those hours, care remains with the parent at home unless separate staffing is arranged. Overnight care is available through After Midnight Engagements.
