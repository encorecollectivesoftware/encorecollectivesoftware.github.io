# Editorial v6 — Services and Pricing

## Design intent
Maintain the credibility of a private Calgary childcare and household-support agency. Present realistic scopes and understandable prices in the primary tiers; introduce the conspicuously premium After Midnight Engagements only after the standard service model is established.

## Changed files
- `services/index.html` — rewritten introduction, Flex/Duo/Family Continuity offers, explicit package inclusions and hours, calm explanatory care process, optional Encore Reserve recurring-membership model, refined After Midnight feature, and service expectations.
- `after-midnight/index.html` — full revision: premium overnight positioning, scope and 8-hour timing, senior two-specialist staffing, clear price construction, schedule and family transparency.
- `assets/css/main.css` — added service-summary panel styling, card spacing and mobile rules.

## Rates (all fictional, CAD)
- Flex: from $69/hour; one specialist; 3-hour minimum.
- Duo: from $129/hour; two specialists as a team; 4-hour minimum.
- Family Continuity: from $2,195/weekend; one lead specialist and up to 20 scheduled hours, not continuous 48-hour care. Additional hours and overnight care are separate.
- Optional Encore Reserve membership: from $295/month for coordination; staffing hours billed separately.
- After Midnight Engagements: from $1,795 for 10pm–6am with two specialists and a morning handover; Private Residence from $3,950 for expanded household coordination, breakfast and restoration.
