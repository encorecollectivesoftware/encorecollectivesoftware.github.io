WEBSITE ANALYTICS

A considered
approach to data.

We offer optional Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this site, including page visits and selected interactions such as opening specialist profiles or viewing services.

Analytics is not activated until you select “Allow analytics.” You can decline or change your choice through Analytics preferences in the footer. The choice may be stored in your browser to remember your preference.

We do not intentionally send the contents of consultation forms, names, email addresses, children's details, or free-text messages to Google Analytics. Google may process technical information associated with permitted website usage, according to its policies.

01 / ENQUIRIES

Information you choose to provide

If the private enquiry form is connected, adult contact details and general care preferences are transmitted to Formspree and the designated form recipient. The page indicates when sending is enabled; otherwise it prepares a local brief only. Do not include sensitive family or children's information.

02 / CONTROL

Your analytics preferences

You may decline optional tracking. If you later withdraw analytics permission, subsequent custom tracking events stop; previously collected information may remain subject to Google's data retention settings. Browser settings and blocking extensions may also affect measurement.

03 / HOSTING

Website delivery

This website is delivered through GitHub Pages. Hosting and technical providers may process routine connection information to serve the pages. Public websites are accessible to anyone with the link.

04 / SERVICE

Care arrangements

Submitting a consultation form or completing the household assessment does not reserve, confirm or purchase care. No payments are collected through this website.
