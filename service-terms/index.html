ENCORE · SERVICE ARRANGEMENTS

Considered care.
Clear expectations.

From occasional assistance to an extended private-residence arrangement, each engagement begins with a shared understanding of the work, the hours and the people involved.

HOW ARRANGEMENTS WORK

Clarity is part
of the service.

Prices shown on this website are starting points for discussing scope, staffing and duration. They are not a guarantee of availability and do not, by themselves, create an agreement.

Any proposed arrangement should be documented before care begins, including the specialist or team, authorized duties, scheduled hours, household requirements, fees and communication arrangements.

RESPONSIBILITIES

What is included—
and what is arranged separately.

01 / CHILDCARE

Within the care brief

Supervision, activities, meals and age-appropriate routines in the agreed location and hours, adapted to each child's stated needs.

02 / HOUSEHOLD

Reasonable support

Cleaning up after meals and activities, children's laundry and shared-space resets only where these responsibilities are included.

03 / ADDITIONAL WORK

Separate agreement

Deep cleaning, specialized meal preparation, driving, off-site supervision, medical tasks, additional children or extra personnel require explicit review and scope.

BEFORE CONFIRMATION

Details settled
in advance.

Formal scheduling is based on a reviewed service brief, not on a website questionnaire or an expression of interest.

Families should be able to examine the proposed specialist's suitability and agree on the practical terms before care is undertaken.

01

Availability & scheduling

Confirm dates, shift length, location, arrival and handover times, and any required overlapping coverage.

02

Fees & changes

Set out the final quote, any deposits, overtime, schedule changes, cancellation window and refund terms in writing.

03

Travel & other expenses

Clarify transportation, parking, meals, supplies and any travel beyond Calgary before they are incurred.

04

Care requirements

Confirm the children's ages, allergies, health instructions, consent arrangements and suitable supervision capacity for the planned activities.

PRIVACY & INFORMATION

A private home deserves
careful handling.

Household information should be limited to what is necessary for planning and safely delivering the agreed service.

  • Do not include sensitive medical details or identity documents in an initial website enquiry.
  • An initial consultation brief may be prepared on the visitor's device, or submitted to the designated recipient if the enquiry form is connected. The form explains which applies.
  • Any later exchange of private family information should use an agreed, appropriate channel.
  • The website does not accept reservations, deposits or payments.
