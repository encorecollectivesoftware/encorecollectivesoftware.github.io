## Brand constants
- Adult fictional women only; no real person or venue implied.
- Editorial photorealism; tasteful glamour; upscale Calgary residential settings.
- Vary focal length, camera height, head tilt, pose and lighting subtly rather than repeating a single glamour portrait pose.
- Target 1400 x 1740 px WebP; no names baked into images; keep subjects clear for card crops.
- The **portrait images have not been regenerated in v3**; this guide is for the one-by-one approval process.
